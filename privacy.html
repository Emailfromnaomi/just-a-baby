What we collect

  • Your email address, so you can sign in. We don't use passwords.
  • What you log: feeds (type and bottle amount), naps, diaper changes, play and baths, with their times.
  • Your baby's profile: the name or nickname you choose, an optional birthday, the portrait options you pick, and your app settings.
  • Who logged what, so caregivers sharing a baby can sync.

We don't collect photos, location, contacts or health records. Please don't type medical information into the app.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to show your log, sync it between the caregivers you invite, and send your sign-in emails. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. During the beta we may count anonymous usage (for example, how many feeds are logged per day) to improve the app. That count never includes what you logged.

Who can see it

Only you and the caregivers you invite. Each invite link works once and expires after 7 days. The owner of a baby can remove a caregiver, and a caregiver can leave at any time.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in [region, for example Canada (Central)]. Sign-in emails are sent through [email provider]. Both act only on our instructions.

Your choices

  • Download everything you've logged as CSV or JSON from the settings screen.
  • Delete your account from the settings screen. This removes your login and every baby you created, with all their entries, immediately. Backups roll off within [N] days.
  • Ask us anything about your data at [contact email].

Not medical advice

The needs bars are a friendly guide built from your own log. They don't diagnose anything or replace your baby's cues or your healthcare provider.
