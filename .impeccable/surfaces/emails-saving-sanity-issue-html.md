# Surface: Saving Sanity newsletter email (Customer.io Design Studio)

Mode: Read. Audience: parents of babies 0–18 months, reading one-handed on a phone during a feed, nap or 3am wake. Job: a 4-minute fortnightly read (parent story, product since the '90s, labelled sponsor spot, parent pleaser). Constraints: email-safe (hosted PNG logo, fallback fonts, light + dark), voice guide, sponsor always labelled, small "from Just a Baby" only in From name and footer. Avoid: soft pastel mommy-blog; looking like Just a Baby app mail; electric-violet + heavy-condensed (AI default, rejected by the user in the slop check).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The issue is the parent's own late-night TV listings: every section is a listing with a fixed deadpan time slot, and the night ends "Off air". Refuses logo + soft cards + pastel.
OWN-WORLD: Off-air black #111111 masthead, white reading page, SMPTE 75% colour bars (#C0C0C0 #C0C000 #00C0C0 #00C000 #C000C0 #C00000 #0000C0) as the only colour, once under the masthead. Black time column in bold tabular Helvetica, hairline #D4D4D4 rules between listings, 3px black rule under the issue strip. Dark: #161616 ground, #1E1E1E page, #ECECEC text. No gradients, shadows, rounded cards or eyebrows above headings.
STORY: The reader recognises late-night TV listings in one glance, laughs at "3:12am · Parent story", reads one story, sees one labelled ad break, gets one thing for themselves.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Black masthead with a large white "Saving Sanity" wordmark (Archivo condensed, PNG) and the tagline "The newsletter for parents that need it." in white beneath; the colour-bar strip; issue strip "Issue 1 · Your listings for the next 2 weeks" over a 3px black rule; first listing: 3:12am + "Parent story" in the left column, H1 title and 16px reading column to the right.
FORM: TV listings digest, candidate 4 of 7 on my ordered list. seed:6bbbe26e552b01c9
Raises: total commitment to two inks plus one signature (from noir one-sheet); colour reserved for the bars and the one action (from drawcord cape).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
